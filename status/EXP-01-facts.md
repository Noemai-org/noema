# Session 2026-11-17 · EXP-01 · explanation/factsource (EL; A155 approved by the Founder, relayed; noema#586 merged)

## Session summary
- What changed: `explanation/factsource` turns typed source rows into the masked, bounded facts and unknowns of explanation-facts.v2 for the seven kinds of the slot inventory (`payment.amount`, `account`, `payment.time`, `payment.counterparty`, `case.ref`, `case.team`, `case.clock`). Only a KNOWN row makes a fact (an INFERRED row gives unknowns: A155); amounts come from integer minor units and the currency table; times use the configured zone (no default); the account shows its last four digits only; the counterparty is the masked form only ([REC], the Founder's call); the case reference loses its year and is masked by `record/payloadguard.Mask`; the team name is pack data; no committed SLA time gives an unknown (A206). Every value is bounded (200) and unchanged by the one digit definition, else an unknown `VALUE_NOT_SAFE`.
- Why: the real FactsSource of A155 (after the Founder's approval of #586): this is its pure, testable core. The reading adapter (the sealed snapshot's items and payloads through `portread` and the PayloadReader, the receipt and the verdicts from the Record) is the next PR; it calls this package.
- A finding for the Architect (noema#586): the display digit definition joins digits across a dot, so an amount with a fraction whose last group has three digits ("NGN 1,234.50": the run `234.50` is five digits) cannot be shown unmasked. It is an unknown (`VALUE_NOT_SAFE`) and the explanation is blocked, never a value with a digit hidden or a cent dropped. Proposed: word such amounts in major units and minor units ("NGN 1,234 and 50 kobo"), the minor unit's name added to `contracts/currencies.json`; or the Founder accepts whole-unit amounts in explanations. Until then those amounts block.
- Tests (REQ-EXP-001, REQ-EXP-002, REQ-SEC-003, REQ-AUD-003; AGENT-13, AGENT-17): the four payment values in the inventory's words and no more than the masked form; minor-unit formatting and the unsafe-amount rule; a missing field is an unknown for its kind only; only KNOWN becomes a fact; no zone, no default; the case facts; a case reference never shows more than four digits (ASCII, Arabic-Indic, spaced and hyphenated).
