## What this means for our contract (read-only observations, no change proposed here)
1. **Confidence is the gap that matters.** Without a per-token confidence the voice clarification flow cannot tell a good key slot from a bad one; by our rules every key slot is treated as low. Whether Intron returns one outside these pages is a question for Intron, in writing.
2. The 3-concurrent-connection and 3-per-minute limits are far below any field-study load and need an enterprise answer before sizing.
3. The 300 s session cap fits an utterance but needs the pipeline to reconnect on long turns.
4. Data terms (superseded by A377 to A379, 19 Nov 2026: Intron's email of 18 Nov is the data terms of record and no further agreement is a precondition for any work, so this is no longer a blocker). Kept as the observation it was: the published policy gives no retention period and lets anonymised data be kept and used indefinitely and shared for research; the account's retention is set to zero days instead.
5. Keeping Intron behind the provider interface, apart from Trust and Execution, is what our architecture already requires (INV-29, A9); these notes change nothing about that.

## Questions to put to Intron (the support address is on the documentation's Introduction page)
Whether the stated limits are defaults or can be raised; the retention period for audio and transcripts and a no-training, no-research commitment in a contract; per-token confidence in any response; the allowed sample rates; per-locale accuracy on Nigerian English and the three code-switched models; latency figures and any SLA; limits at enterprise tier; retention, training use and residency for audio and transcripts; the effective date and scope of the terms.
