## 1. What counts, and what does not

G1 needs all four conditions of the gate record, shown, none inferred: US-01 and US-06 run end to end on text through the public API; the unknown outcome verified by key and never retried; the Record reconstructs both; the property tests for INV-03, INV-07, INV-08 and the prohibited registry green. Plus the security conditions (items 5 to 12 below): any one failing fails G1.

**Does not count:** anything before G0 passes (both halves); a run on a head that is not the final head; a retried run presented as the first; a partial run; a "not run" presented as a pass; component-level e2e tests in place of the public-API run (they stay in the package as CI evidence); any claim stronger than "tamper-evident". **Nothing in code enforces any of this.** The preconditions and the rules below are prose: no check stops a dispatch, or the counting of a run, before G0 passes. The control is people: the Benchmark Owner's READY TO DISPATCH post and the Founder's single dispatch, read by the Security Reviewer. Nobody should read G1 as code-enforced. **The data label stays:** enterprise data is the stand-in (to be rerun against live Tushe), and the model side is the replay provider (no live call, no provider key). Both are written on separate lines in the package.

## 3. Dispatch preconditions: when BO posts READY TO DISPATCH

BO posts **READY TO DISPATCH at <sha>** on noema#436 (and noema#117) only when **all nine** of these are merged on `main` at that SHA and every check run at that SHA is green, read by BO from the check runs of that commit on `main`:

1. The stack workflow (noema#444) and IL's follow-up that passes the four `--idp-*` flags and a throw-away key pair.
2. The Trust lane: C1's assembly of T1 to T15 (noema#567, #570 and #571 are merged; noema#572, T13 to T15, is open) and EL's `Trust.Decide` adapter (A186 lookup included), recording and grants in one transaction. The adapter is EL's and depends on C1's T10 to T15.
3. The write lane: `RunWrite`, the Executor and Verifier ports and their adapters (noema#576, open; Codex, SR and an independent read are still open on it), **and, separately, the composition PR that registers the worker in `cmd/core`** (not #576).
4. The read lane: the `Interpreter` **adapter** over the merged Interpreter (noema#519 is the Interpreter itself; the adapter is C2's, with four design points for EL on noema#565), the Evidence adapter (noema#548, merged), **the Reconciler (not built: its payload reader is noema#599, merged; the Reconciler itself is C2's next slice, noema#596, with the evidence mapping noema#589)**, the Admissions, Explainer and Recorder adapters (noema#558, #559, #562, merged), `POST /v1/conversations` and the investigation and audit routes mounted, and `/readyz` ready. **`POST /v1/conversations` is not mountable until the Architect decides noema#601** (CONFLICT DETECTED: what it answers conflicts between PERD 39.2 and the Input Gateway), so G1 item 1 cannot run through the public API before that.
5. **The compiler behind the compile route.** The route is merged (noema#544) and the first slices are merged (noema#581 the assembler, #582 the pack loader, #584 the lexicon). The compiler that joins them and calls a provider is **noema#598, open**. Until it is merged the route answers 503 and US-01 cannot run; it also stays 503 until noema#555 closes.
6. **The model side is the replay provider, so it must exist.** The replay provider (UND-15) needs recorded compile output from the compile-acceptance run (noema#546, ruling A198), which has not run. If neither exists at the dispatch head, the model side cannot be the replay provider, and the data label cannot be written.
7. **mTLS between the core and the model service** (noema#555: noema#563, the core's client, is merged; noema#568, the model service's listener, is open; both must agree; nothing sets `compile_service` before it is closed, the Security Reviewer's condition on #544).
8. The steps SR's items 6, 8 and 9 need, in the workflow or the runner (noema#591 is the open work for 6, 8 and 9; noema#577 carries the leak scan and the token flags): the FaultPlan and its seed recorded, the leak scan with its planted canary, the two negative probes.
9. G0 passed, both halves (G1 evidence counts only once it does).

If any of these is missing at the dispatch head, the right outcome is "G1 not met, with this list", not a weaker run. Nobody dispatches before that post; the Founder dispatches once (this role's token has no Actions write permission), from the SHA in the post.
